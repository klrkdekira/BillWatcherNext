Methodology and limitations

BillWatcherNext is an unofficial, read-only index. Parliament of Malaysia is the authority. We preserve timestamped source observations and link to official records.

Normalized statuses are careful classifications, not legal conclusions. A chamber marking a bill passed does not establish royal assent, gazettal, commencement, or current law.

Document bytes are stored privately for preservation and re-extraction; this site links to official documents while republication rights remain under review.
